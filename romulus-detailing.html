Important policies / FAQ Required
  • Prices shown are estimates and may change after photos or inspection.
  • A-1 is monthly maintenance only for new/like-new vehicles or vehicles already reset by Romulus Detailing.
  • Stains, odor, salt, pet hair, smoke, scratches or neglected interiors do not qualify for A-1.
  • Full Detail is standard interior + exterior cleaning. It does not include deep interior rescue or exterior polish/buff by default.
  • The Works includes deep interior + one-step paint enhancement. It improves shine and light defects but does not guarantee full scratch removal.
  • Heavy correction, deep scratches, wet sanding, severe oxidation or ceramic coating correction may require custom quote.
  • No absolute guarantee of 100% stain, odor, scratch, oxidation or prior-damage removal.
  • All vehicles are worked on after 5:00 PM.
  • Same-day delivery only if the procedure allows.
  • Specialized work may require a full Saturday or Friday + Saturday/Sunday.
  • Custom quote recommended for very dirty vehicles, biohazard, mold, smoke, heavy pet hair, severe stains, heavy paint correction, ceramic coating or multi-day jobs.
  • Romulus Detailing may reject mold, blood, feces, urine, pests, flooded interiors, unsafe vehicles, loose/broken parts, aggressive customers or unrealistic expectations.
  • Large jobs, weekend jobs or multi-day jobs may require a deposit. Deposits reserve time and are applied to the final total.
  • No appointment is confirmed until Romulus Detailing confirms price, day, time and conditions by message.
